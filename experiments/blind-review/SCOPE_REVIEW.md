## Locality of qualifications

The most consequential caveats are generally well placed: no pretrained outer weights, predecoded code, stock prefixes only, tiny guest size/memory, selection-set quality, single-run timing, reference-seeded windows, first frame only, and projected interactive rates. Improve three Doom sentences (129, 137, 139), retain those qualifications, and do not turn this review into a universal-equivalence or full-stock-inference endorsement.
